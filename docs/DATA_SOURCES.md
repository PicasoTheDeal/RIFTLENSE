# Data sources

All require nothing more than a free NASA Earthdata login unless noted.

## Earth terrain and surface
NASA SRTM 30 m and NASADEM elevation: https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov
ASTER GDEM and ASTER L2 surface reflectance (mineral indices): https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov
MODIS land surface temperature MOD11, NDVI MOD13, albedo MCD43: https://appeears.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov (AppEEARS lets you clip and download a small area as CSV or GeoTIFF without touching raw HDF)
GPM IMERG rainfall: https://giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov (Giovanni gives time averaged maps in the browser)
NASA Worldview for quick visual checks and screenshots: https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov

## Radar
ASF Vertex (Sentinel-1 and NISAR search and download, plus HyP3 on demand InSAR and RTC processing): https://search.asf.alaska.edu
NISAR mission data portal: https://nisar.jpl.nasa.gov/data
NASA ARIA share (ready made coseismic interferograms for major events): https://aria-share.jpl.nasa.gov

## Fire and thermal
FIRMS archive download (MODIS and VIIRS CSV by country or box): https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/download
FIRMS map and API for recent data: https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/map

## Moon and Mars targets
Mars MOLA elevation and THEMIS day and night infrared (USGS Astrogeology): https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search
Mars Trek: https://trek.nasa.gov/mars
Moon LOLA elevation and Diviner temperature: https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu
Moon Trek: https://trek.nasa.gov/moon
NASA PDS for Jezero and south pole site studies: https://pds.nasa.gov

## Context layers
OpenStreetMap for railway, roads, towns (Geofabrik Ethiopia extract): https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/ethiopia.html
Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program for Erta Ale, Dallol, Fentale, Dofan records: https://volcano.si.edu
