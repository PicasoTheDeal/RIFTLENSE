# Pitch outline (4 minutes, 7 slides)

1. Cold open. Photo of the ground cracks across the Addis to Djibouti railway, early 2025. "This is not an earthquake. This is Ethiopia becoming an ocean, and it is the closest thing to Mars on this planet."
2. The problem. NASA wants new, under studied Earth analogs for Moon and Mars bases. The best one is in our backyard and nobody has characterised it with data. Meanwhile the people living on it get no warning when it moves.
3. The idea. RIFTLENS: one map, three layers. Analog score, radar deformation, thermal pulse.
4. Live demo part one. Global analog ranking map, click Dallol, scorecard appears, compare to Atacama. Show where the analogy breaks.
5. Live demo part two. Radar time slider over Fentale, ground bulges before cracks appear. Fire calendar shows Erta Ale as a permanent heat source correctly separated from Bale fires.
6. Why it matters. For NASA: a systematic method plus a new analog site. For Ethiopia: a free hazard monitor for the rift corridor and a case for hosting international analog missions in Afar.
7. Team and next steps. N0xCod3, data used, what we would build with two more weeks, link to the live app.
